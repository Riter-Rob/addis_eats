# Addis Eats — Product Truth

## Purpose & Cultural Home
Addis Eats brings authentic, handcrafted Ethiopian cuisine directly to homes, workplaces, and gatherings across Addis Ababa. Rooted in the rich culinary heritage of Ethiopia, Addis Eats celebrates the ritual of sharing food (Gursha), slow-simmered clay pot stews, freshly ground berbere, and fragrant Jebena coffee culture.

## Audience
- **Local Residents & Professionals:** Seeking quick, dependable lunch and dinner deliveries in commercial hubs like Bole, Kazanchis, Piassa, and CMC.
- **Diaspora & Visitors:** Looking for trustworthy, authentic Ethiopian tastes prepared with high culinary standards.
- **Fasting / Vegan Diners:** Relying on strict traditional fasting (Tsom) preparation, including Beyaynetu, Shiro, and lentil-based dishes.

## Operating Context & Constraints
- **Delivery Geography:** Bole, Kazanchis, Piassa, Kirkos, 4 Kilo, Megenagna, CMC, Sarbet, and Old Airport.
- **Currency:** Ethiopian Birr (ETB) with clear tabular numeral formatting.
- **Payment Affordances:** Cash on Delivery, Telebirr, CBE Birr.
- **Speed & Freshness:** Hot dishes packaged in thermal-insulated containers to preserve the heat of fresh clay pot stews and injera.

## Brand Voice & Principles
- **Warm & Welcoming:** Generous, hospitable, and warm like traditional Ethiopian mesob dining.
- **Artisanal & Craft-focused:** Celebrating earthenware, spices, fresh rosemary, and traditional slow-cooking methods rather than impersonal fast food.
- **Accessible & Honest:** Clear pricing, honest preparation times, and accessible interfaces for everyone.
